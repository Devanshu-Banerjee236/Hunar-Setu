Slide 5 Architecture

Trust and Safety: Built so workers are protected

Four foundational security pillars ensuring rural employment dignity and scam immunity.

🛡️

Listings checked first

Nothing reaches a worker until it passes verification. Listing Agent audits enterprise credentials, GSTIN/MSME, and wage parity against district statutory minimums.

🔒

Consent before sharing

Worker details go to an employer only after approval. Phone number and village location are tokenized and protected until the job seeker explicitly authorizes contact.

⚖️

Bias control

Skill scoring follows a fixed rubric, not appearance. Facial contours, dress, regional dialect, and gender are blinded from the model; scoring is strictly kinematic and task-based.

👤

Human in the loop

Low-confidence scores and rejected listings get reviewed. Orchestrator automatically escalates borderline videos (dim lighting) or ambiguous wages to human reviewers.

📋 Questions We Ask in Field Interviews

•
How did you find your last job? (Discovered: 78% relied on middleman agents taking 1st month salary).
•
Why did the last hire leave? (Discovered: 45% skill mismatch, 35% wage not paid as promised).
•
What does an employer check? (Discovered: Practical tool handling matters 10x more than paper diplomas).
•
How far, and for what wage? (Discovered: Workers prefer <20 km unless free lodging is provided).
•
Has a fake listing cheated anyone? (Discovered: 32% of youth lost ₹800–₹2,500 in advance registration fee scams).
•
What does a bad hire cost? (Calculated below: avg ₹28,000 lost in machine downtime and re-hiring).
